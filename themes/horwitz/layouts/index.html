Horwitz Lab

We study the neural mechanisms of visual perception and visually guided behavior in macaque monkeys, with a focus on how cone photoreceptor signals are transformed along the LGN–V1 pathway to support spatial and color vision.

Our Research Publications Lab Members

Research Areas

We combine Neuropixels electrophysiology, behavior, viral vector–mediated gene delivery, and optogenetics to dissect the cellular and circuit mechanisms of vision.

Color & Spatial Vision

How cone signals are combined in the LGN–V1 pathway to encode chromatic and luminance contrast.

Flicker Perception

What limits the temporal resolution of vision and how early visual circuits process time-varying stimuli.

Eye Movements

How visual signals guide saccadic eye movements and what the cerebellum contributes to their control.

Viral Vectors & Optogenetics

Developing and validating AAV vectors targeting specific cell types for causal circuit interrogation.
